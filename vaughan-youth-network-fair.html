Vaughan Youth Network Fair

Connecting Students with Opportunity and Purpose

The Vaughan Youth Network Fair brings together youth, local organizations, and community leaders in one interactive space. Designed to empower students, the fair connects participants with volunteer opportunities, leadership programs, and community resources to help them gain necessary experience and make a positive impact.
