Who We Are

The Vaughan Youth Council is a youth-led initiative created to address the lack of accessible opportunities for young people to engage and lead civic activities.

In 2025, our council was established because it became clear that many young people wanted to participate, but the spaces that already existed were often controlled by adults or shaped by particular political agendas. Youth understand their own needs and challenges best, and when they lead the work, they are more likely to stay involved and contribute long-term.

Since our inception, we have organized and launched community projects designed to tackle local issues, foster youth advocacy, and drive positive change across Vaughan. In 2026, we also formed a formal partnership with the Rotary Club of Woodbridge to expand our impact through mentorship and shared resources. By giving young people true ownership over community involvement, we are creating a supportive environment where local youth can contribute, lead, and shape their community for years to come.

Our Values

Our work is grounded in three core pillars that shape every initiative we create.

01

Advocacy

We believe in elevating the voices of youth on critical issues such as sustainability, education, and social awareness. By producing digital campaigns on social media and hosting conversations with community leaders, we hope to bring these vital discussions to the forefront.

02

Growth

Personal and professional development is at the heart of what we do. We help young people build essential skills, create meaningful connections, and become active contributors to society through engaging workshops and social gatherings.

03

Giving Back

We believe in the power of selfless service. We are dedicated to supporting local non-profits and leading community projects, like neighborhood cleanups, giving youth direct opportunities to uplift their community.
